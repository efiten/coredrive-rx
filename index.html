Keep capturing with the screen off

Some phones, OnePlus for one, close apps in the background to save battery, even while the capture notification shows. Capture then stops without a warning.

Before you drive, open the phone's Settings > Apps > CoreDrive RX > Battery and choose Unrestricted, or turn off battery optimisation and allow background activity. The names differ per brand; dontkillmyapp.com shows them for yours.
