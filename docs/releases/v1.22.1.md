# CoreDrive RX v1.22.1

The advert signature check now turns on when config.json asks for it, and a backlog of an earlier companion is no longer lost at the broker.

## What's new

- **`verifyAdverts: true` works.** The flag was dropped while config.json was read, so the
  Ed25519 check on adverts never ran, whatever config.json said. It is now kept.
- **A backlog of another companion waits for that companion.** Rows captured by an earlier
  companion were published under its pubkey while the MQTT clientId was the companion
  connected now. With the ACL bound to `${clientid}` the broker denies that, and with
  EMQX's default `deny_action: ignore` it still acknowledges it, so the rows left the
  queue without being stored. They now stay queued until their own companion is connected,
  and the debug log says how many are waiting.
- **README:** the EMQX ACL section recommends `meshcore/client/${clientid}/#` and lists all
  five subtopics (`/packets`, `/rf`, `/regions`, `/names`, `/uplink`). It listed three.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v58`.**
- **Advert verification:** a deployment with `verifyAdverts: true` in config.json now really
  drops the identity of an advert whose signature does not check out. Leave it out (or set
  `false`) to keep the behaviour of v1.22.0.
- **Queue:** rows of a companion that never connects again stay in the queue; nothing removes
  them automatically.
- **ACL:** a broker that lists subtopics one by one needs `/names` and `/uplink` added, or those
  publishes are dropped.
- The MQTT payload did not change.
- Tests: 430 pass (+2).
