# CoreDrive RX v1.18.4

Fixes a CoreScope link that kept dropping itself after "Push pending now" was pressed
during a slow connect.

## What's new

- **Fix: "connected", "offline" and "connection closed" in the same second, repeatedly.**
  On a disconnected uplink, "Push pending now" forces a reconnect through mqtt.js
  `client.reconnect()`. mqtt.js `connect()` replaces its stream without closing the old
  one, so a press while a CONNECT was still unanswered left the earlier socket open next
  to the new one. When such an orphaned socket closed, its `close` event marked the whole
  client disconnected while the newest socket held a live session, and the reconnect that
  followed orphaned that session in turn.

  A field log from 2026-09-19 shows the sequence: 30 s connack timeouts on a weak mobile
  link, then five presses in four seconds (12:05:54 to 12:05:57), then "connected"
  followed by "offline" and "connection closed" in the same second at 12:05:58, 12:06:00,
  12:06:02 and 12:06:04. 34 records went out at 12:06:02, and the uplink was down again a
  second later.

  "Push pending now" now leaves an attempt that is already in flight alone and logs "a
  connection attempt is already under way — not starting a second one". Between attempts,
  after the socket has closed, it still skips the 4 s wait as before. The in-flight
  attempt ends by itself within mqtt.js's 30 s connect timeout.

  `test/publisher.test.mjs` runs the real mqtt.js client over a fake socket and pins both
  cases: no second socket while a CONNECT is unanswered, and a forced attempt once the
  socket has closed.

## Upgrade notes

- Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v53`.
- No configuration or data migration. The MQTT payload contract is unchanged.
