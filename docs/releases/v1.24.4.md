# CoreDrive RX v1.24.4

The Android app keeps uploading with the screen off.

## What's new

- **Screen off no longer stops the Android app.** In v1.24.3, about a minute after the screen
  went off, uploads, RF samples, region asks and uplink pings stopped until the screen came back
  on, while receptions were still recorded. Three field logs of 2026-11-11 show gaps of 7 to 25
  minutes. The app now keeps its page running while the screen is off.
- **New log lines.** The debug log says "screen off or app in the background, capture goes on"
  and "app on screen again" in the Android app.

## Tested

- One phone (OnePlus, Android app), stationary, screen off for about 4 minutes, once on USB and
  once on battery: uploads went on for the whole time and the CoreScope link stayed up.
- Not tested: region asks and uplink pings with the screen off, a screen-off drive, other phones,
  battery use.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v64`.**
- The web app (Chrome, Bluefy) is unchanged apart from the cache bump and the wake lock listening
  for one more event. It still pauses in the background.
- No data, config or MQTT payload change.
