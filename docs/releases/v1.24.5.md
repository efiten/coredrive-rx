# CoreDrive RX v1.24.5

RF samples start again within 15 seconds when you drive off after a stop.

## What's new

- **RF sampling resumes at once after a stop.** While parked the app samples the radio every 5
  minutes. When you drove off, the sample already scheduled on that 5-minute timer still had to
  run out first, so the first minutes of the drive had no RF samples (a log of 2026-11-11 shows
  4 min 19 s). Moving again now brings the next sample forward to 15 seconds.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v65`.**
- No data, config or MQTT payload change.
