# CoreDrive RX v1.18.2

A repeater saved as a contact no longer holds up the queue after it has already answered.

## What's new

- **The contact-override hold ends on the reply.** When a repeater is a saved contact,
  its stored path is forced to zero-hop for the ask and put back afterwards, and the
  queue waits for that round. It used to wait a flat 20 seconds. In the 2026-09-14 log
  `49a98584fce5` and `72d171e63056` both answered two seconds after the ask, and the
  queue then sat blocked for another 18 seconds each; `cafe314d00e2`, heard at 17:47:01,
  was not asked until 17:47:14 because of it. Every answer visible across the field logs
  so far — eleven of them — arrived one to two seconds after its ask.

  The hold now ends the moment that repeater's reply is matched. When no reply comes, the
  20-second fallback is unchanged: how early a restore is safe before an answer has
  arrived is a firmware question this release does not guess at.

- **The restore line says which way the hold ended**, so a field log can confirm it:
  `regions: restored 72d171e63056…’s original path (reply in, hold ended early)`, or
  `(no reply in 20s)`.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v51`.**
- No config or contract change. The bonus ask is unchanged and stays in.
