# CoreDrive RX v1.14.0

The v1.13.0 field log shows what was left: six region requests in twelve minutes, one
answer. The one that was answered fired on a live reception and came back in two
seconds. Of the other five, two never reached the radio at all — the BLE characteristic
went invalid on a reconnect and the write threw — and three were fired by the 60-second
timer at repeaters last heard three to six minutes earlier. All five charged their target
an attempt and dropped it into the 5/15/30-minute retry backoff, so repeaters that were
being heard right then could not be asked. This release removes the timer and makes a
failed write cost nothing.

## What's new

- **Region requests are now sent only while the repeater is being heard.** The 60-second
  timer fallback is gone. It picked a target from the whole session pool with no notion
  of range, which at road speed means a repeater you have already passed; every such ask
  in the field log went unanswered. Worse than the wasted airtime, each one spent the
  shared one-ask-per-60s budget and pushed its target into the retry backoff, so the ask
  that could have been answered was blocked by the one that could not.

- **A request that never leaves the phone no longer costs the repeater anything.** When
  a BLE write throws — the characteristic goes invalid on a reconnect, or the companion
  drops mid-round — the attempt, the retry clock and the airtime budget are refunded and
  the in-flight slot is released immediately. Previously the target was charged for a
  packet that was never transmitted and the round was logged 20 seconds later as
  `no reply … within 20s`, which reads as a repeater that heard us and stayed silent.
  Two of the six asks in the field log were this.

- **The log says why an ask did not happen.** With the timer gone there is no
  once-a-minute `nothing to ask` line, so the heard path now reports its own verdict, at
  most once a minute: `regions: heard 14036f132198… but not asking —` followed by the
  ask in flight, the seconds left on the shared airtime budget, the remaining per-target
  backoff, or that the repeater already declared its list this session. Those four were
  previously indistinguishable from each other and from silence.

- **A dropped companion no longer wedges a round.** If the transport disappears between
  scheduling a request and sending it, the round is now abandoned and logged instead of
  rejecting unobserved and holding the slot until it times out.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v42`.** Clients pick up the new bundle
  on their next load with a connection.
- **No MQTT contract change.** No new field and no new topic, so no ingestor work is
  needed. The `regions` record shape is unchanged.
- **No config change.** `regionDiscovery` keeps its existing default.
- **Fewer requests per drive, aimed better.** The ceiling is unchanged at one ask per 60
  seconds, but asks now only happen on a reception, so a quiet stretch sends nothing
  instead of sending into the void.
- **A parked app depends on organic traffic.** The stationary pause already stops the
  discover sweep, so while parked the only candidates are repeaters whose own traffic is
  heard: a forwarded FLOOD packet or an advert. A repeater in range that transmits
  nothing while you are parked next to it is no longer asked, where the timer would have
  asked it. This is the one case the removed fallback covered.
- **A candidate heard exactly once is no longer asked later.** If the ask is blocked at
  that moment — budget spent, or a request in flight — that repeater is asked on the
  next reception, and if there is no next reception it is not asked at all.
- Bundle 443.94 kB to 444.14 kB, gzip 140.12 to 140.21.
- Tests: 278 pass (+2 net; 7 timer-scheduler tests removed, 9 added).
