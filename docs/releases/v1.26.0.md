# CoreDrive RX v1.26.0

RF samples every 100 m while driving, a clearer first step in the Android app, and a smaller Android app.

## What's new

- **RF samples by distance.** While moving, the app used to sample the radio every 15 seconds.
  At 90 km/h that is 375 m between samples, more than one hexagon on the CoreScope map from
  zoom 13 on, so the noise layer showed gaps along a road. A sample now follows every 100 m
  driven, and at most 15 seconds apart when you go slower. Parked stays at every 5 minutes.
  An RF sample is a question to the companion over Bluetooth: it sends nothing on air.
- **Clear first step in the Android app.** Until you choose a deployment, Settings shows a
  blue card with the steps: open your CoreScope on another screen, open **Coverage** in its
  menu, and scan the QR code there or type the address under it. Once chosen, the card shrinks
  to one line that says where the app uploads.
- **Scanning the deployment you already use changes nothing.** It used to restart the app,
  which dropped the companion link and cleared the debug log. Switching to another deployment
  while connected now asks first.
- **Smaller Android app.** The app is now built with R8: the APK went from 6.45 to 3.35 MB.
  Google Play also gets the matching mapping file, so crash reports stay readable.
- **Update check in the debug log.** The Android app writes one line per start with what the
  update check saw, also when there is no update.

## Tested

- 498 automated tests.
- The R8 build on a OnePlus phone: Bluetooth, upload, RF samples with the screen
  off, region asks, QR scan and log sharing work.
- Not tested on a phone: RF samples by distance while driving, the new deployment card, and
  scanning the deployment already in use.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v67`.**
- More RF samples reach CoreScope while driving fast: at 90 km/h about 3.75 times as many.
- No data, config or MQTT payload change.
