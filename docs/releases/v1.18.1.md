# CoreDrive RX v1.18.1

Fixes the link-state lines that v1.18.0 added, which fired the wrong way round.

## What's new

- **Fix: "companion link back" once a second on a healthy link, and never "link
  down".** The guard in `noteLinkState` was inverted. The stored flag holds the inverse of
  the link being up, so the steady state is `linkQuiet !== linkUp` and an edge is
  `linkQuiet === linkUp`; the guard returned on the edge and fell through in the steady
  state. Every tick of a working link announced a return, and the flag never left its
  initial value, so a real drop could not be announced at all.

  A field log from 2026-09-14 shows 157 of the 200 buffered lines spent on it, the whole
  buffer covering 2 minutes 36 seconds, and not one "link down" line. The heard, asks
  and regions lines had rolled out underneath.

  The edge decision is now the pure `linkTransition` in `src/monitor.js`, with tests for
  both steady states, both edges, and a full drop-and-return cycle.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v50`.**
- No behaviour change beyond the log. The link gate itself (discover, RF sampling and
  region asks standing down while the companion is gone) was correct in v1.18.0; only
  its two announcements were wrong.
- Tests: 275 pass (+5).
