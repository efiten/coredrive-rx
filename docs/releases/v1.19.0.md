# CoreDrive RX v1.19.0

Log in to your CoreScope account from the app, and every companion you connect is linked to it.

## What's new

- **CoreScope account card (Settings).** It appears only when the CoreScope this app
  uploads to has user management with companion linking on (`/api/config/client` →
  `userManagement.enabled` and `userManagement.companionLinking`).
  Log in with your CoreScope email and password; the app keeps a device token, never the
  password, and the token is never shown or logged. The card shows who is logged in and
  the companions linked to the account, with ● on the one connected now. **Log out**
  revokes the token on the server too.
- **Companions link themselves.** When you connect a companion that is not linked yet,
  the app asks CoreScope for a challenge, has the companion sign it with its own key
  (MeshCore `CMD_SIGN_*`), and sends the signature back. The companion then shows up under
  **Companions** on your CoreScope account page and its coverage counts as yours. Firmware
  that cannot sign is named on the card; a refused link shows the reason and a **Retry**
  button. While the companion signs, region discovery, the discover sweep and the RF sampler wait, so their
  replies cannot be mistaken for the signature's.
- **Uploads wait when CoreScope only accepts linked companions.** If the server has
  `clientRxCoverage.requireLinkedCompanion` on, it drops data from unlinked companions
  after the broker has already accepted it. The app now keeps that data in its queue
  instead, says "Log in to contribute your data – N waiting", and sends everything once
  the companion is linked (a few seconds later: CoreScope needs that long to see a new
  link). With the setting off, which is the default, uploading is exactly as before and
  never waits on the account.
- **Every queued record goes out under the companion that captured it.** Until now a
  backlog was published under whichever companion was connected when it drained, so
  receptions from one companion could be counted for another. Records queued from this
  version on carry their own companion.
- **The MQTT payload did not change.** Same topics, same bytes, logged in or not.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v54`.** The worker no longer caches
  CoreScope's `/api/config/`, `/api/auth/` and `/api/account/` answers.
- **`corescopeUrl` in `config.json` is optional.** Without it the app uses the origin of
  `resolveUrl`. Set it when `resolveUrl` points at a proxy rather than at CoreScope
  itself. With both empty the account feature stays off.
- **Different origins:** when RX is served from another origin than CoreScope, add the RX
  origin to CoreScope's `corsAllowedOrigins`. Without it the debug log says
  `account: off — … unreachable … corsAllowedOrigins …` and the app behaves as before.
- **Needs CoreScope with companion linking** (user management sub-project F) and
  `userManagement.enabled`. Against an older CoreScope, also one that already has
  accounts, the card stays hidden and uploading works as before.
- **Upgrade every RX client before an admin turns on `requireLinkedCompanion`.** Older
  versions keep publishing, and CoreScope then drops their data.
- Tests: 379 pass (+96).
