# CoreDrive RX v1.21.0

Uplink ping: the app can now test whether the mesh hears your companion, not only what your companion hears.

## What's new

- **Uplink ping (off by default).** When `uplinkPing` is on, the app sends a short message on
  a dedicated hashtag channel (`#coredrive` by default) containing only a random tag, such as
  `cdrx 0a1b2c3d`. Your position goes to CoreScope over MQTT, never over the mesh.
  - For 10 seconds after each ping, the app listens for a repeater re-broadcasting it. An
    echo proves a repeater heard you, and its first hop says which one.
  - Each ping is published on the new topic `meshcore/client/{pubkey}/uplink` (type
    `UPLINK_PING`) with the tag, the packet hash of the echo when there was one, every echo
    heard and your position. CoreScope can match it against what its observers received.
- **Region scope.** `uplinkScope` (e.g. `"be"`) sends each ping as a region-scoped flood, so
  only repeaters configured for that region forward it. The override is reset immediately
  after the ping. Needs companion firmware v8 or newer; with an older firmware and a scope
  set, pinging stays off and says why.
- **Paced to keep airtime down.** A ping goes out only with a GPS fix of 100 m or better,
  while moving, and only when all of these hold:
  - at least `uplinkIntervalSec` (60) since the last ping
  - at least `uplinkMinMoveM` (250 m) from the last ping
  - fewer than `uplinkMaxPerHour` (50) in the last hour
  - not in a ~180 m cell already pinged in the last `uplinkSkipDays` (7) days. This
    "smart skip" is remembered on the phone across sessions.
- **Channel slot.** On connect the app looks for the ping channel on the companion and,
  if it is missing, adds it to the first empty channel slot. A slot holding another channel
  is never overwritten; with no free slot, pinging stays off and the debug log says so.
- **Debug log header:** a `ping` row (status, pings sent, echoed, failed) and `uplinkPing`
  in the config row.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v56`.**
- **New optional config keys:** `uplinkPing` (default off), `uplinkChannel`, `uplinkScope`,
  `uplinkIntervalSec`, `uplinkMinMoveM`, `uplinkMaxPerHour`, `uplinkSkipDays`. See
  `config.example.json`.
- **CoreScope does not store `/uplink` yet.** Until its ingestor handles the topic, the
  messages are published and ignored. The payloads on `/packets`, `/rf`, `/regions` and
  `/names` did not change.
- **New dependency:** `@noble/ciphers` 2.0.1, for the AES-ECB step that recognises the
  ping's echo (WebCrypto has no ECB).
- Tests: 417 pass (+20).
