# CoreDrive RX v1.28.0

A red warning when the companion link drops, and Home times that keep counting while it is down.

## What's new

- **Red banner while the companion link is down.** When the Bluetooth link to the companion
  drops, a red banner at the top of every screen says how long it has been down and to restart
  the companion if it does not come back. It disappears as soon as the link is back. Before,
  the only sign was a grey "Discover paused" line on Home, which was easy to miss: on a drive
  of 2026-11-24 the link was down twice, for 10 and 4+ minutes, without being noticed.
- **Home times keep counting while the link is down.** "Last reception", "upload ... geleden"
  and pkt/min stood still at the moment of the drop, so the last reception looked recent. They
  now keep updating.

## Tested

- 506 automated tests, 3 of them new for the banner text.
- The banner layout in a headless Chrome render.
- Not tested on a phone: the banner during a real link drop.
