# CoreDrive RX v1.16.0

The 2026-09-12 drive answered the question v1.15 was built to ask, and the answer was no.
A signal floor would have cost answers, not saved airtime. What the same log did show is
a repeater that spent its whole allowance during a weak stretch and then was heard 46 dB
stronger with nothing left to ask. That is what this release fixes.

## What's new

- **No signal floor, and the measurement that killed it.** The `asks sig` ranges came
  back overlapping: answered spans snr -5.25 … +12 dB, silent spans -11 … -0.75 dB. Two
  of the three answers came from asks at snr -5.25 / rssi -117 and snr -1.75 / rssi -113,
  which any floor worth setting would have blocked. The earlier reading that suggested a
  clean split was drawn mostly from the old scheduler, where silent asks were timer asks
  whose "signal" came from a reception minutes earlier — the number was standing in for
  freshness, not for link quality.

- **A much stronger reception buys one extra ask.** A repeater can spend its encounter
  allowance in a bad spot and still be in front of you when the signal improves. In the
  field log `efef79435050` used its three asks between 21:06:50 and 21:08:08, all around
  -115 dBm and all unanswered, then was heard from 21:09:37 at -76 to -69 dBm — the best
  receptions of the drive — with nothing left to spend. A reception at least 6 dB better
  in snr than the best one this encounter already asked on now earns exactly one more
  ask. Once per encounter, and configurable (`regionBonusSnrDb`).

- **The per-encounter allowance goes from 3 to 4.**

- **The repeater's own limiter is now enforced directly.** At most four asks to one
  repeater in any 180-second window, which is what `simple_repeater` accepts across all
  requesters and request types. It was previously approximated by the cap and the
  spacing; with the cap raised and a bonus on top it has to be explicit. It ages on its
  own clock, so an encounter reset does not clear it, and it is not configurable — it is
  not ours to raise. On the road it rarely comes up: a repeater is usually out of range
  long before four asks, and in a 12-minute log only nodes heard for minutes at a time
  ever reached it.

- **The header counts all three outcomes**, since a capped repeater, one held off by the
  limiter, and one that earned an extra ask are three different things:

  ```
  asks cap   96 receptions ignored (encounter allowance spent), 4 held off by the repeater limiter, 2 extra asks earned by a much stronger reception
  ```

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v46`.** One load with a connection picks
  it up.
- **No MQTT contract change.**
- **New optional config key** `regionBonusSnrDb` (default 6), and `regionMaxAsks` now
  defaults to 4.
- **The default cap fills the limiter window.** Four asks 30 seconds apart is four in any
  180-second window, so a repeater that uses its whole allowance cannot be given its
  bonus ask until the oldest one ages out — about half a minute later in the case above.
  Setting `regionMaxAsks` to 3 in `config.json` leaves the fourth slot free for the bonus
  instead; no rebuild needed.
- Bundle 444.70 kB to 445.64 kB, gzip 140.44 to 140.83.
- Tests: 264 pass (+7).
