# CoreDrive RX v1.27.0

A Stop & close button in the Android app, the update note on every screen, and a debug log that covers a whole drive.

## What's new

- **Stop & close (Android app).** The phone's Back and Home buttons leave capture running in
  the background, and Disconnect sat on Settings, so there was no easy way to end the app. A
  red **Stop & close** button at the bottom of Home now stops everything and closes the app.
  While connected it asks first. Receptions not uploaded yet are kept and go out the next time
  you connect. The capture notification points to the button.
- **Update note above the tabs (Android app).** The note that a newer version is on Google
  Play now sits above the bottom tabs on every screen. It used to show only on Home, while the
  app opens on Settings until a deployment is chosen.
- **Debug log keeps 2000 lines instead of 200.** At the rate of a drive with full RF logging
  (about 19 lines a minute) that is about 105 minutes; 200 lines covered about 10. A full
  export is about 140 kB.
- **Debug log: a line when Disconnect is pressed**, so a log that stops shows whether the user
  ended the capture.
- **Debug log: the reconnect line no longer says the timers were frozen.** It now reads
  "reopening the CoreScope link from a reception".

## Tested

- 503 automated tests.
- The 2000-line log on a OnePlus phone (a test build reported 213 lines kept).
- Not tested on a phone: the Stop & close button, the update note above the tabs, and the two
  new log lines.

## Upgrade notes

- **Service-worker cache bumped to `coredrive-rx-v68`.**
- The Android app has one more Capacitor plugin, `@capacitor/app`, for closing the app.
- No data, config or MQTT payload change.
